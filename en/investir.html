Rhône-Azur — a new district in Briançon

58,700 m², 350 homes, a park and a hotel: regenerating the Sanatorium site.

Résidence Orma by Icade

One- to four-bedroom apartments 300 m from the Prorel lifts, delivery Q3 2027.
