Services

Sell, buy, value

Four ways to work together, depending on where you stand in your project.

Selling a property

A reasoned, written valuation, a cadastral check where the boundaries are not marked, a photographic and technical file, targeted marketing (fellow agents, eXp networks, international buyers), systematic viewing reports and a weekly catch-up.

Agency fees: payable by the buyer, without exception on businesses for sale and commercial premises.

Get a valuation

A free, no-obligation valuation, delivered in writing: value range, local comparables, strengths and sticking points, works to consider before going to market, realistic selling time.

Request a valuation

Buying

A short selection rather than a catalogue: I show you what genuinely matches your project, including properties that are not publicly listed. Review of the local plan, of constraints (listed buildings, UNESCO, protected areas) and of service charges before any offer.

Investing

Commercial freeholds, businesses for sale, building land, new-build, seasonal lettings and rental yield. I build the files with trading figures and verifiable numbers.
