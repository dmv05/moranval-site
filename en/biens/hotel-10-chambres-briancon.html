Freehold and Business — 10-Bedroom Hotel, Serre-Chevalier Briançon

€715,000

Freehold of an 11-bedroom hotel with 1 fully equipped restaurant. Terrace. Kitchen renovated in 2016. One-bedroom staff flat, 1

office, self-contained. Ski room and bike room. 1 large car park and 2 small car parks. Land.

-------------

Seize the opportunity to acquire a charming hotel ideally located in the dynamic Serre-Chevalier Briançon resort. This rare property includes both the freehold and the business, offering a turnkey solution for a demanding investor.

The hotel has ten comfortable bedrooms with a warm atmosphere, complemented by a friendly breakfast room and a private car park — a major logistical asset in this sought-after area. Its exceptional visibility on a strategic route guarantees a steady flow of guests and immediate recognition.

The hotel's early availability allows the business to be taken over without delay, ideal for preparing the coming season. Above all, this investment offers exceptional upside thanks to the 2030 Winter Olympic Games, bringing international visibility and lasting rental growth potential. Whether you wish to continue the spirit of this characterful place or take it upmarket, this confidential address perfectly combines Alpine authenticity and economic profitability. This small hotel close to the town centre awaits its new owner to write a new chapter in its history at the summit of the Alps.
