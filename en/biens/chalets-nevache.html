Mountain-View Chalets with Swimming Pool — Névache, Vallée de la Clarée (05)

€2,000,000

Nestled in the heart of the Clarée valley, a listed and protected natural site since 1992, this true Alpine jewel in Névache offers a preserved and authentic setting, bathed in sunshine and pure air — one of the finest skies in the Southern Alps.

Discover this magnificent 839 m² house, renovated in 2018, combining elegance and comfort. With its 14 rooms including 14 bedrooms (most with private shower rooms), served by a lift, this spacious property is a haven of peace.

🌅 Picture yourself waking to the first rays of the rising sun lighting up the surrounding mountains. Unobstructed views, immaculately maintained interiors, double glazing and individual heating for optimum comfort all year round. Outside, a private pool invites you to relax facing the peaks. The house is free, unfurnished and ready for your project.

A unique place, with two major projects possible:

  • 👨👩👧👦 Multigenerational living: 14 self-contained bedrooms with shower rooms — each generation has its own space under the same roof
  • 👵 Senior living: easy single-storey access, lift, bedrooms with private bathrooms, and Briançon hospital just 20 km away for peace of mind

An exceptional environment:

  • 🏞️ Preserved valley, larch forests, lakes and hiking trails setting out from the house
  • ⛷️ 10 km from Montgenèvre, a competition venue for the 2030 Winter Olympics (French Alps) — the Briançonnais area has been officially selected and Briançon will host the Olympic village
  • 🎿 Névache cross-country ski area (45 km of trails), Serre-Chevalier and Montgenèvre nearby, Italian border close by
  • ☀️ Exceptional sunshine, pure mountain air, absolute calm

Close at hand, every practical convenience to make daily life easier. Do not miss this rare opportunity to live in an idyllic setting, between protected nature and Olympic momentum.
