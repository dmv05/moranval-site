Retail Premises in Le Monêtier-les-Bains

€1,090,000

Commercial premises — 516 m² of potential

Discover these spacious 516 m² commercial premises in a strategic location, ready to be turned into a thriving retail space. With 180 m² on the ground floor, this property offers a unique opportunity for visionary entrepreneurs.

Picture yourself creating a dynamic, welcoming retail space where every detail is designed to attract and retain customers. The large timber openings add a warm, authentic touch, while the double-glazed doors ensure optimum insulation.

The premises comply with ERP standards (public-receiving establishment), meaning they are ready to welcome the public safely.

Although the interior needs some refreshing, this is an ideal opportunity to tailor the space to your specific needs. The common areas also require some minor works, but with a little imagination you can create a welcoming, functional environment for your customers and staff.

Benefit from shared parking to make access easy for your customers and staff. Extraction is also available, a major asset for businesses requiring efficient ventilation.

Do not miss this unique opportunity to turn these commercial premises into a thriving, attractive retail space. Contact us today for more information and to arrange a viewing.

Amenities: several amenities are within a 10-minute drive, including restaurants, schools and, above all, the departure point of the Serre-Chevalier Monêtier ski area.
